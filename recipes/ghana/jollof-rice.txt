GHANAIAN JOLLOF RICE

Serves: 5–6

INGREDIENTS
- 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice, rinsed
- 3 large tomatoes, or 1 can (about 400 g) chopped tomatoes
- 1 red bell pepper
- 1 hot pepper, such as Scotch bonnet, to taste
- 1 large onion, divided
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 thumb-sized piece of fresh ginger
- 3 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1/3 cup neutral cooking oil
- About 2 cups chicken stock, plus a little hot water if needed
- 1 teaspoon curry powder
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

METHOD
1. Blend the tomatoes, bell pepper, hot pepper, half the onion, garlic, and ginger until smooth.
2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot. Chop and cook the remaining onion until softened. Add the tomato paste and fry for 2 minutes.
3. Pour in the blended mixture. Simmer uncovered for 20–30 minutes, stirring now and then, until reduced and thick.
4. Stir in the curry powder, thyme, bay leaf, salt, and pepper. Add the rinsed rice and stir to coat it in the sauce.
5. Pour in enough stock to just cover the rice. Cover the pot tightly and cook over low heat for 25–35 minutes. Check occasionally; if the rice is still firm and the pot is dry, add a small splash of hot water or stock.
6. When the rice is tender, turn off the heat and let it rest, covered, for 10 minutes. Remove the bay leaf and fluff gently before serving.

Serve with fried plantain, chicken, or a fresh salad.
