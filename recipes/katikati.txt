KATI KATI — CAMEROONIAN GRILLED CHICKEN IN PALM-OIL TOMATO SAUCE

Serves: 4
Preparation: about 25 minutes
Cooking: about 45 minutes

INGREDIENTS
- 1.2 kg bone-in chicken pieces, or 1 small chicken cut into pieces
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne or ground hot pepper, more to taste
- 1 seasoning cube, optional
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- 3 tablespoons red palm oil
- 3 ripe tomatoes, chopped or blended
- 1 small onion, chopped or blended
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 100–150 ml water or chicken stock, as needed

METHOD
1. Pat the chicken dry. Rub it with salt, onion powder, garlic powder, white pepper, cayenne, optional seasoning cube, and neutral oil. Let it stand for 15–20 minutes.
2. Grill or broil the chicken, turning to char it lightly on all sides. Cook until nearly done; the chicken will finish in the sauce. If you do not have a grill, brown it in a heavy pan.
3. Heat the palm oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the tomato and onion and cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring, until softened. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes.
4. Add the chicken and any juices to the pot. Add a little water or stock if the sauce is too thick. Cover and simmer gently for 20–30 minutes, turning the chicken once, until it is fully cooked and tender. The thickest part should reach 74°C / 165°F on a food thermometer.
5. Taste the sauce and adjust salt and pepper. Serve with corn fufu and njama njama (garden huckleberry greens), or another leafy vegetable.

NOTE
A little char from grilling gives kati kati its smoky flavor. Keep the pepper level to your taste.
