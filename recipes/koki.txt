KOKI — CAMEROONIAN STEAMED BLACK-EYED PEA CAKE

Serves: 4
Preparation: about 30 minutes, plus soaking
Cooking: about 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours

INGREDIENTS
- 500 g dried black-eyed peas (cowpeas)
- 150–200 ml red palm oil
- 1 small hot pepper, minced or pounded (optional)
- Salt, to taste
- About 200 ml lukewarm water, as needed for blending
- Banana leaves, softened over a flame; or foil if leaves are unavailable

METHOD
1. Rinse the peas and soak them in hot water for about 2 hours. Rub them between your hands to loosen the skins. Rinse several times, removing the floating skins, until most are gone.
2. Drain the peas. Blend them in batches with a little lukewarm water until you have a smooth, thick paste. Use only enough water to help the blender move.
3. Warm the palm oil gently until liquid; do not let it smoke. Gradually whisk it into the bean paste. Add the pepper and salt, then mix until the paste is evenly orange and fluffy.
4. Pass each banana leaf over a low flame to soften it. Spoon a portion of paste into the center, fold into a leakproof parcel, and tie or secure it. If using foil, wrap each portion securely.
5. Set the parcels in a pot above a rack or a layer of spare leaves. Add water below the parcels, cover, and steam over medium-low heat for 90 minutes to 2 hours. Check occasionally and add hot water if the pot is drying out.
6. Carefully open one parcel to check that the center is firm and fully cooked. Steam longer if it is still soft or pasty. Serve hot with boiled plantain, yam, or cassava.

NOTE
Koki should be fully cooked through; cooking time depends on the parcel size and steamer. Banana leaves give the traditional aroma.
