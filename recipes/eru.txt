ERU — CAMEROONIAN ERU AND WATERLEAF

Serves: 6
Preparation: about 30 minutes
Cooking: about 1 hour 15 minutes

INGREDIENTS
- 400 g finely shredded eru leaves (Gnetum africanum)
- 800 g waterleaf, chopped; or spinach with a handful of sorrel as a substitute
- 400 g beef, cut into small pieces
- 200 g cow skin (kanda), cleaned and sliced (optional)
- 200 g smoked fish, deboned and flaked
- 2 tablespoons ground dried crayfish
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 150–200 ml red palm oil
- 1 hot pepper, minced (optional)
- Salt, to taste
- 1 stock cube, optional
- Water, as needed

METHOD
1. Put the beef and cow skin in a pot with the onion, salt, optional stock cube, and about 500 ml water. Cover and simmer until tender, about 45–60 minutes. Keep the cooking liquid in the pot.
2. While the meat cooks, put the chopped waterleaf in a separate pot and cook for about 5 minutes, until it releases liquid and wilts. Drain well, pressing out excess water.
3. Add the shredded eru and drained waterleaf to the meat pot. Stir, cover, and cook for about 15 minutes so the eru softens.
4. Add the palm oil, smoked fish, ground crayfish, and optional pepper. Stir well and cook uncovered for 15–20 minutes, stirring often. The greens should be tender and glossy, with little free liquid. Add a small splash of water only if the pot is sticking before the greens soften.
5. Taste and adjust the salt and pepper. Serve hot with water fufu, garri, or another cassava accompaniment.

NOTE
Eru is a fibrous leaf and should be shredded finely. If using dried eru, soak it first and allow extra cooking time. Waterleaf helps soften the dish; spinach can be used when waterleaf is unavailable.
